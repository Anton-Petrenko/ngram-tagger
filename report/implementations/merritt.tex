\subsubsection{Implementation}

In terms of differences between this approach and the rest, the approach developed 
by Matthew and Michael Merritt has a few changes in the hopes of lowering data
preprocessing time as well as using additional failsafes to resolve ties with the
goal of obtaining better accuracy. For the changes to reduce data load times,
this approach utilizes a pandas dataframe which can have functions mapped onto the
entire dataframe more efficiently rather than having to iterate row by row and apply
the functions individually. Regarding the additional failsafes, this approach tries
to resolve ties within the non-unanimous $n$-grams that arise in the training process 
by failing down to the next smallest $n$-gram to break the tie more accurately as
opposed to just randomly picking one of the tied options. This tiebreaking improvement
was implemented because it was observed that the vast majority of incorrect guesses
our approach was making was due to these aforementioned ties, resulting in a
significantly lower accuracy than expected.
